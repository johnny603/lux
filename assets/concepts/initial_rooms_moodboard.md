# Visual Concepts & Moodboards

Initial room moodboards and thematic color stories for Lux escape rooms:

## 1. Abandoned Lab (`room_1`)
- **Tone**: Eerie, cold fluorescent, forgotten research facility, bioluminescent remnants.
- **Palette**: Deep Navy (`#0b132b`), Cyan Glow (`#6fffe9`), Slate Gray (`#415a77`).
- **Interactive Props**: Terminals, chemical flasks, keycards, microscope slides.

## 2. Ancient Tomb (`room_2`)
- **Tone**: Dusty stone, flickering torchlight, hieroglyphic engravings, sand particles.
- **Palette**: Sandstone (`#d4a373`), Obsidian Black (`#1c1917`), Amber (`#f59e0b`).
- **Interactive Props**: Relic urns, stone tablets, bronze mirrors, lever mechanisms.

## 3. Cyberpunk Alley (`room_3`)
- **Tone**: Rain-slicked asphalt, vibrant neon signage, holographic glitches, exposed conduits.
- **Palette**: Neon Purple (`#b5179e`), Electric Blue (`#4cc9f0`), Charcoal (`#121212`).
- **Interactive Props**: Fiber-optic cables, vending machines, cyberware jacks, spray-painted hints.

## 4. Enchanted Library (`room_4`)
- **Tone**: Infinite towering bookshelves, floating constellation orbs, velvet upholstery, soft candle glow.
- **Palette**: Royal Indigo (`#311b92`), Parchment Cream (`#fefae0`), Gold leaf (`#ffd700`).
- **Interactive Props**: Grimoires, astronomical globes, feather quills, locked display cabinets.

## 5. Steampunk Workshop (`room_5`)
- **Tone**: Brass gears, steam valves, analog pressure gauges, warm incandescent bulbs, walnut wood.
- **Palette**: Antique Brass (`#c68b59`), Copper Patina (`#2a9d8f`), Rust Brown (`#6c584c`).
- **Interactive Props**: Pressure levers, cogs, chronometers, pneumatic tubes.
